Sources and method

Member rankings, futures prices and option quotes: Dalian Commodity Exchange data as republished by Sina Finance (the exchange's own site only serves a browser that passes its bot check). Updated every weekday evening, Malaysia time. Option volume history is rebuilt from each strike's daily bars. Option open interest is published only as the current figure, so its history on this page starts on 27 November 2026 and grows by one day each trading day. Net position history adds up all tracked contracts. Not financial advice.
