Now to December 2026Neutral to bearish ▼Central RM4,200–4,450
January to June 2027Bullish ▲Central RM4,350–4,850 · El Niño case up to RM5,650

Bottom line: heavy now, tighter next year. Record Malaysian stocks and well-stocked buyers cap prices into year-end; the seasonal output drop and a strong El Niño point to tighter supply in the first half of 2027.

SupplyHeavy now

Malaysian stocks hit a record 3.62 Mt in October. Output has started its seasonal fall in November.

WeatherSupports 2027

DemandMixed

IndonesiaCompetitive
