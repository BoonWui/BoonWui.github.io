Vegetable Oil Price Map

The same oil has a different price depending on where and how it changes hands: free on board (FOB) at the export port, or delivered with freight (CFR, also called CNF) and insurance (CIF) at the buyer's port. The delivery terms

FOB, free on board

Price at the exporter's port, loaded on the ship. The buyer pays freight and insurance from there.

CFR or CNF, cost and freight

Price delivered to the buyer's port, freight included, insurance not. CNF is the older name for CFR.

CIF, cost, insurance and freight

Like CFR, plus marine insurance. For a buyer, CIF minus the origin FOB price is roughly freight and insurance, if grade and timing match.
