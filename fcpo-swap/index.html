FCPO Swap Calculator

Two ways to swap palm oil price exposure. An average-price swap trades a fixed price for the monthly average FCPO price over a period. A month roll moves a futures position from one FCPO month to another.

Broker markup

The broker adds this to the fair fixed price when the client buys the swap, and takes it off when the client sells. If both boxes are filled in, RM/t is used.

Month by month (blank = expected average above)

Each month settles in cash: (monthly average − fixed price) × tonnes. A palm oil swap usually averages the daily settlement of the 3rd-month FCPO contract; check the reference in your own deal. Starting values are an example, not live market data.

Fair fixed price = the fixed price that makes the swap worth zero today: the discounted average of the expected monthly prices. Expected prices are your inputs, for example from the FCPO futures curve. Not trading advice.

A roll is two trades at once: close the near month and open the same position in the far month. One FCPO lot is 25 t. Starting values are an example, not live market data.

What it means

Roll gain or cost compares the new far-month price with the near-month price you leave: what you give up or gain by keeping the position for longer. It is not a fee, and the final profit depends on where the far month ends. Not trading advice.
