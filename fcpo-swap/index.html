FCPO Swap Calculator

Two ways to swap palm oil price exposure. An average-price swap trades a fixed price for the monthly average FCPO price over a period. A month roll moves a futures position from one FCPO month to another.

Hedge funding

–

The cash a broker ties up as margin on the futures hedge. Cost a month = (initial + variation margin) × rate ÷ 25 t ÷ 12. It is taken off the bid and added to the ask, times the months until the middle of each swap month.

Broker markup

The broker adds this to the fair fixed price when the client buys the swap, and takes it off when the client sells. If both boxes are filled in, RM/t is used.

Quote from the FCPO futures curve

Type today's FCPO bid and ask for each contract month, or paste a row or column from Excel into the first box. A swap month's floating price is the average of the 3rd-month FCPO during that month: the contract 2 months later until the 15th, then the one 3 months later. So swap month = ½ (M+2) + ½ (M+3), e.g. Dec = ½ Feb + ½ Mar. Gaps are filled from the months either side.

Fair fixed price = the fixed price that makes the swap worth zero today: the discounted average of the monthly floating prices. Floating prices are your inputs: actual averages for months that are over, the FCPO futures curve for the rest. Not trading advice.

A roll is two trades at once: close the near month and open the same position in the far month. One FCPO lot is 25 t. Starting values are an example, not live market data.

What it means

Roll gain or cost compares the new far-month price with the near-month price you leave: what you give up or gain by keeping the position for longer. It is not a fee, and the final profit depends on where the far month ends. Not trading advice.
