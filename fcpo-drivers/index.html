How the score works

Each factor reads +1 (bullish), 0 (neutral) or −1 (bearish) from its latest data. Score = sum of weight × reading, from −100 (every factor bearish) to +100 (every factor bullish). +15 or more = bullish, −15 or less = bearish, in between = neutral. The weights are judgement, checked against the data below: rival oils and energy were raised and Malaysian stocks and exports lowered after the test on 27 November 2026 showed soybean oil explains far more of FCPO's moves than first assumed.

How the weights were tested

Re-tested automatically on the 1st of every month with the latest data.

Correlation runs from −1 to +1; "explains" is the share of FCPO's weekly or monthly moves that the factor alone accounts for (R²). Market factors are tested on weekly returns; MPOB figures on the FCPO change in the month their report comes out. A short sample and correlation, not proof of cause. Factors without enough history (Dalian prices, positioning, options) are not tested yet.
