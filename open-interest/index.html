Veg Oil Open Interest

Open interest is the number of futures contracts still open. Rising open interest with a rising price means new money is buying; rising open interest with a falling price means new selling. Falling open interest means positions are being closed. Chinese markets are daily totals across every contract month; US and ICE markets are weekly, from the Commitments of Traders reports.

About the data

Daily (China): Dalian palm olein and soybean oil (DCE) and Zhengzhou rapeseed oil (ZCE), summed over every listed contract month from Sina Finance daily bars. Updated every weekday evening.

Weekly (US and ICE): CBOT soybean oil, soybeans and soybean meal from the CFTC Commitments of Traders (futures and options combined); ICE gasoil from ICE’s Commitments of Traders. Positions as of each Tuesday, published on Friday (ICE updates its free history file less often).

Not included: Bursa Malaysia FCPO. Bursa does not publish total open interest free of charge. See the BMD FCPO Monitor for price, trend and volatility, and DCE Palm Broker Positions and CFTC Fund Positions for who holds the positions.
