DCE Palm Oil Broker Positions

Every trading day the Dalian Commodity Exchange publishes the 20 brokers (member firms) with the largest long and short positions in each palm oil contract. This page follows who is building or cutting positions, and how that lines up with the price.

Sources and method

Data: Dalian Commodity Exchange daily member rankings (成交持仓排名) and daily prices for palm oil futures, as republished by Sina Finance. The exchange's own site only serves these tables to a browser that passes its bot check, so they are taken from Sina instead. Contracts: the near month plus the next January, May and September contracts. Updated every weekday evening, Malaysia time, for the last six months. Groups add up their members. A broker's position is the total of all its clients' positions through that broker. Rankings only list the top 20, so a broker that drops out of the list has no figure that day. Sina's copy misses an occasional day (for example 20, 23 and 24 November 2026); those days show no bar and the line skips them. One lot = 10 tonnes. Not financial advice.
