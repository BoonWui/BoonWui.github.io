How it works

  • Reference price. Each month Indonesia's Ministry of Trade sets one CPO reference price from three markets: Indonesia (KPBN), Malaysia and Rotterdam. If all three are within US$40 of each other, it takes a weighted average: Indonesia 60%, Malaysia 20%, Rotterdam 20%. If any two are more than US$40 apart, it drops the outlier and averages the two closest prices.
  • Bracket. The reference price falls into a US$50 bracket (bracket 1 is up to US$680; bracket 7 is US$931–980). Duty is a fixed US$ amount per bracket and product (PMK 38/2024, amended by PMK 68/2025; same amounts as PMK 123/2022).
  • Levy. A percentage of the reference price that funds the plantation fund (biodiesel). Since 1 March 2026: CPO 12.5%, crude olein and PFAD 12%, refined oil, olein and stearin 10%, packed olein up to 25 kg 7.25%.
  • Why it matters. A lower levy plus duty makes Indonesian oil cheaper abroad, which tends to take export demand from Malaysia.
