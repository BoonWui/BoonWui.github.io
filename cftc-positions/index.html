CFTC Fund Positions

Every Friday the US Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) shows how traders were positioned on the Tuesday. "Managed money" is the hedge funds and commodity trading advisers: the speculators whose buying and selling often drives the big swings. This page tracks their long, short and net positions in 25 commodities, including the soybean oil that palm oil trades against.

Sources and method

US markets: CFTC Public Reporting, Disaggregated Commitments of Traders, futures and options combined. Brent and Gasoil trade in London, so they come from ICE Futures Europe's Commitments of Traders, same format. These are the same managed-money figures that terminals such as LSEG show. Positions are as of Tuesday and published on Friday at 15:30 US Eastern (early Saturday in Malaysia); US holidays delay them a few days. This page checks for new data every Saturday and Monday. "Chg %" is the change in net position against last week's net. 52-week high and low are of the net position. Not financial advice.
