World Palm Oil Demand

Who buys the world's palm oil, and how much. India is the biggest buyer and China the second; together with Pakistan and the European Union they take nearly half of all palm oil traded. The figures are the US Department of Agriculture's monthly supply and demand estimates, the most widely used free source, including its forecast for the current season.

Sources and method

Data: USDA Foreign Agricultural Service, Production, Supply and Distribution database (public domain), downloaded twice a month and refreshed after each WASDE report. A season such as 2026/27 runs October to September for most countries. The latest season is a forecast and changes month to month. China's monthly customs figures for 2025–26 are not yet in the free UN Comtrade database, and port stock surveys are paid data, so this page uses USDA's season totals. Monthly India figures from the importers' association are on the India imports page. Not financial advice.
