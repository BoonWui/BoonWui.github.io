Method

Production: last month's figure times the usual change into this calendar month (average of the last five years). Exports: half that same seasonal rule, half last year's figure for the month scaled by the year-on-year trend of the last three months. Domestic use and imports: the average of the last three months. Formula change, November 2026: forecasts up to October 2026 used last year's month times the recent trend for both production and exports. Over 2020–2026 back-tests the new formula cut the average stock miss from about 188k to 167k tonnes and called the direction right 53 times out of 82 instead of 46. The October 2026 forecast in the track record keeps the old formula it was made with. End-month stocks = last month's stocks + production + imports − exports − domestic use. Stock-to-usage = stocks ÷ (exports + domestic use). Likely range: the estimate plus the 10th to 90th percentile of the misses (actual minus estimate) over the last 36 back-tested months. Track record: when MPOB publishes a month, the next month's forecast is saved to a log and never edited, then scored once MPOB reports it. Not included: cargo surveyor export data (ITS, AmSpec), which traders use for exports and which is not free to republish, and weather. See the Weather Monitor for rainfall. Release date: MPOB usually publishes around the 10th of each month at about 12:30 pm Malaysia time; a weekend date moves to the next working day. Actual dates can differ.
