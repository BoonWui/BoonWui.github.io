Sources and method

Prices: Yahoo Finance via the open-source yfinance library, adjusted for dividends and splits; usually about 15 minutes delayed. The 62 companies are large S&P 500 members chosen to cover all 11 sectors, not the full index. Sector performance uses the Select Sector SPDR ETFs (XLK, XLF, XLV, XLY, XLC, XLI, XLP, XLE, XLU, XLRE, XLB). Concentration uses RSP (equal weight) against SPY (cap weight). Volatility is the Cboe VIX; rates are the 10-year Treasury yield (^TNX). Returns use trading days: 1 week = 5, 1 month = 21, 3 months = 63, 1 year = 252. Volatility is the annualised standard deviation of daily returns; beta is measured against the S&P 500 over one year; Max DD is the largest fall from a peak over one year; RSI is the 14-day Wilder index. Trend is "Up" above a rising 50-day average that is above the 200-day, "Down" for the reverse, otherwise "Mixed". A GitHub Actions job refreshes the latest prices every hour while US markets are open and rebuilds the full history after the close. Prices are not live.
