BMD FCPO Monitor

Crude palm oil futures on Bursa Malaysia Derivatives (FCPO) are the world benchmark for palm oil. This page tracks the third-month contract that the market quotes, reads its trend and momentum, and checks them against what the big brokers in Dalian and the funds in US soybean oil are doing.

Sources and method

FCPO daily prices and volume: Bursa Malaysia Derivatives, third-month benchmark contract, via Sina Finance (Bursa's own site blocks automated access). Closes are within a few ringgit of Bursa's settlement. Dalian palm oil: DCE Palm Broker Positions. US funds in soybean oil: CFTC Fund Positions. Updated every weekday evening after the Bursa close. The read scores seven signals at +1 (bullish), 0 or −1 (bearish): price against its 20-day average, the 50 against the 200-day average, RSI(14), MACD, a 20-day breakout, the Dalian top-20 brokers' net flow over 5 days, and US funds' weekly change in soybean oil. Total of +2 or more = bullish, −2 or less = bearish. Not financial advice.
