FCPO Model

Every palm oil driver this site collects, lined up week by week in one table and fed to one model. It learns from the data how much each driver has mattered, gives the chance that FCPO is higher in four weeks, shows why, and keeps score of every call it has made. Method and sources

Weekly table (Fridays, 2020 onward): FCPO (Bursa, via Sina); CBOT soybean oil, Brent and USD/MYR (Yahoo Finance); MPOB stocks, production and exports from the 10th of the following month; El Niño index (NOAA ONI) from the 10th after each season; US funds in soybean oil (CFTC) on the Friday they are published. Each driver is only used from the day it was actually known. Model: regularised logistic regression on standardised drivers (penalty chosen by out-of-sample test). Walk-forward: from 2022 every week is a true out-of-sample call. Raw probabilities were over-confident, so each is shrunk towards 50% by a factor learned only from earlier, already-scored calls. Updated every week. Not financial advice.
