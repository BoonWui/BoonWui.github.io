Palm Oil Weather Monitor

Rain is the first link in the palm oil supply chain. Dry spells cut fruit bunch output 6 to 12 months later, so this page tracks rainfall against normal in the eight main oil palm belts of Malaysia and Indonesia, plus the next 16 days.

Sources and method

Weather: Open-Meteo (ERA5 reanalysis archive and forecast models), licensed CC BY 4.0. Each region is one representative point in its oil palm belt, so local rain can differ. The normal is the 2001–2020 average for each calendar month. The latest days of the archive lag by a few days; the current month is compared with the normal for the days so far. Production: MPOB monthly data, via the Palm Oil Market Monitor. A GitHub Actions job rebuilds the data every morning. Not financial advice.
