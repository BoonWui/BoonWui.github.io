Every 15-minute window, at minute 5 and minute 10, the page writes down the model’s chance of Up and the order book’s mid price, then scores both against the result (Brier score: lower is better). The model only has an edge if it beats the market’s own price, by enough to clear fees. A study of 768 past windows (2 days, 4 coins, 28 Nov 2026) found no difference: Brier 0.188 model vs 0.189 market. Keep this page open to build your own record; trade real money only when the gate opens.

Methodhow the bot prices
  1. Realized vol: each 15-min window's variance = sum of its fifteen squared 1-min log returns.
  2. HAR: ln RV(t+1) = b0 + b1·last + b2·1h + b3·1d + b4·time-of-day, fitted on the first 70% of history.
  3. Student-t: returns ÷ forecast vol fitted to a t with ν degrees of freedom (fat tails).
  4. Fair odds: P(Up) = Tν(move ÷ σ left); the final minute counts ⅓ because settlement is a 60-second average.
  5. Trade: buy a side when fair − (ask + fee) ≥ min edge; buy both when Up + Down asks + fees < $1 (locked pair); hedge a one-sided position when the other side now completes a pair under $1.

Fee ≈ rate × p × (1 − p) per share, less rebate (about 2.5¢ near 50/50). Prices from Binance; settlement uses the Chainlink oracle, which can differ. Paper only, no wallet. A learning tool, not trading advice. The public account this mimics netted about half its $336k pre-fee profit after fees on $18M traded.
