Bursa Plantation Monitor

Malaysia's benchmark FBM KLCI and nine listed plantation companies, tracked every trading day. It shows how each stock has moved, how risky it has been, how closely it follows the market, and how much it tracks palm oil prices.

Sources and method

Stock and index prices: Yahoo Finance, via the open-source yfinance library, adjusted for dividends and splits. They may be delayed and are shown for educational purposes only. Palm oil: World Bank Commodity Price Data (Pink Sheet), CC BY 4.0, from the Palm Oil Market Monitor. Volatility is the annualised standard deviation of daily returns over one year. Beta is the slope of daily stock returns on KLCI returns over one year. Year to date runs from the last close of the previous year. A GitHub Actions job refreshes the data after Bursa closes each weekday. Not financial advice. Nothing here is a recommendation or an offer to buy or sell securities. Past performance does not predict future returns.
