Indonesia Palm Oil Tracker

Indonesia produces more than half the world's palm oil, about 56 million tonnes of crude palm and kernel oil in 2025. The Indonesian Palm Oil Association (GAPKI) publishes a monthly balance about two months after the month ends. This page collects those figures, each linked to its release, and sets Indonesia's stocks beside Malaysia's.

Sources and method

Data: GAPKI monthly press releases (Indonesian), gapki.id. Production is CPO + palm kernel oil; exports cover all palm products including oleochemicals; domestic use covers food, biodiesel and oleochemicals; stocks are CPO + PKO. GAPKI sometimes restates a month in a later release; the note column says where. Missing: November and December 2025 had no monthly release, and January 2026 stocks were not stated. July 2026 stocks are as reported by Kontan citing GAPKI. Updated by hand after each GAPKI release, usually about two months after the month ends. Malaysia: MPOB via the MPOB Nowcast, updated automatically. Not financial advice.
