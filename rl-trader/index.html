Features that rarely change, such as monthly MPOB figures carried forward, count for less: about 700 days hold only a few dozen monthly releases, so their correlations are less reliable. IC is the correlation between the feature today and the FCPO return over the chosen horizon. The agent scores features on the training period only, keeps those whose IC has the same sign in both halves of it, and drops any that are more than 80% correlated with one it already has. Weight is how much the trained policy leans on the feature when choosing long over short.

    What this is. A learning demo on real daily data, not trading advice. Risk controls apply to every model: a stop-loss (exit and wait 5 days if a position loses the STOP %), a drawdown cut (go flat for 10 days after the book falls DD CUT % from its peak), slippage that grows with volatility, and a roll cost each month for holding futures. The agent is a Q-learning reinforcement learner. Under MODEL you choose its value function: Linear (a weighted sum of the features, easy to explain) or Deep (DQN), a small neural network with two hidden layers of 32 units, replay memory and a target network. kNN finds the past days whose features look most like today and follows what happened next; k-furthest is an experimental contrarian that bets against what happened after the least similar days. With a DQN, kNN or k-furthest the Weight column shows the average sensitivity of long-versus-short to each feature. Each day it chooses short, flat or long one FCPO position for the next session, and pays the cost you set on every change. It trains on the first 62% of days, picks its checkpoint on the next 18%, and is scored on the last 20%, which it never sees while learning. Monthly MPOB and World Bank numbers only enter on the day they would have been published. About 700 days is a small sample, so expect results to change with the seed. The FCPO series from mid-October to early November 2026 mixes in the November contract, as noted on the fundamentals page.
